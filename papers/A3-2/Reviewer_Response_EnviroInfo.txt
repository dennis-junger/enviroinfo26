REVIEWER 1

Spatially blocked cross-validation.
Reviewer 1 asked for a spatially blocked scheme to firm up the generalisation
claims. A preliminary leave-one-group-out evaluation was added to Section 4,
holding out one monitoring group per fold. The pooled out-of-group R2 falls below
the global-mean baseline, indicating that the random cross-validated performance
partly reflects group identity shared between training and test sites. This is
framed as an exploratory robustness check rather than a rigorous generalisation
estimate, since the number of monitoring groups is small and C_residual carries a
group-specific level that a held-out group cannot recover. The generalisation
caveat in the Discussion was updated accordingly.

Variance partitioning.
Reviewer 1 also asked for a quantitative variance partitioning of group structure
versus water quality. Within the work-in-progress scope this analysis was not run
but added to the future work in Section 6. The existing ablation (full 0.719,
no-group 0.718, water-quality-only 0.388) reported in Section 4 serves as the
current partial decomposition.


REVIEWER 2

Perceived limited novelty.
Reviewer 2 found the study competent but not distinctive. The contribution was
sharpened rather than extended with new methods. The Introduction now states the
contribution explicitly as a spatial-resolution diagnosis with consequences for
monitoring-network design and maintenance planning, rather than the headline R2.
The Conclusion now separates assessment for monitoring groups represented in the
data, which the results support, from prediction at unobserved locations, which
the leave-one-group-out check suggests is substantially harder. Together with the
added leave-one-group-out evaluation, this reframes the study around its
actionable diagnostic finding.


CONSISTENCY CORRECTION

Independent of the reviews, the group and parent counts in Section 3 were
corrected to match the actual model dataset, which comprises five monitoring
groups and 39 corrosion parents (previously stated as six and 34) carrying all
153 measurement sites. The reference to median-based water quality summaries in
Section 6 was corrected to mean-based, matching the methodology.
